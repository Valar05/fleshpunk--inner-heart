# Vanguard Origin Campaign Bible — Inner Heart

Status: WIP campaign spine built from current Inner Heart canon + first-party Vanguard of Glory transfer ore.

## Mission Boundary

This campaign follows a younger Vanguard alone inside the existing Fleshpunk: Inner Heart setting.

Do not introduce the later crew by default.
Do not make Chorus her companion or command authority.
Do not replace Inner Heart factions, characters, room logic, economy, heartbeat, cloning architecture, or delayed adaptation with a new dungeon cosmology.
Do not write Vanguard as already mythic.

The campaign should make her become Vanguard through practical survival, local reputation, debt, combat learning, social friction, gear state, food, repair, and repeated contact with a country made out of a body.

## Campaign Premise

Vanguard enters the Inner Heart without a crew and without being sent by Chorus.

Her initial objective is materially simple: get through, recover what she came for, survive the route, and leave with enough gear and body left to matter.

The Inner Heart complicates that because it does not treat visitors as neutral persons. It classifies them.

The first campaign question is therefore not "can Vanguard kill the dungeon?"

It is:

**What category of living thing does the Inner Heart decide Vanguard is, and what does she do with the privileges and debts attached to that category?**

The body-country can classify her as:
- traveler
- edible inventory
- contract-capable debtor
- repair labor
- tolerated predator
- route asset
- weapon
- heir to an abandoned operator role
- specimen
- rival claimant

Vanguard resists abstraction by demanding practical terms:
what does the mark buy, who recognizes it, where can she sleep, who repairs armor, what happens if she refuses, and what exactly is owed.

## Prime Writing Rule

Write consequences, not events.

Every important scene should preserve state:
- debt
- route recognition
- visible damage
- gear damage
- hunger
- body odor / scent classification
- repair status
- social reputation
- Merchant claim
- faction access
- armor cracks
- weapon position
- enemy structural damage
- learned movement
- local hospitality
- witnessed violence

A choice is not finished when the button resolves. Something in the country should carry it forward.

## Young Vanguard

Preserve these first-party traits:

- capable, unfinished
- practical before ceremonial
- curious about systems that affect survival
- willing to comply with local procedure until it becomes extraction
- dry rather than theatrical
- enjoys good food
- notices prices
- maintains gear
- can be impressed
- can be technically outclassed
- makes expensive mistakes
- learns by watching economical fighters
- does not confuse armor with invulnerability
- can owe people without immediately treating debt as humiliation
- becomes locally known before becoming legendary

Her main growth axis is not raw power.

It is **economy**.

Early Vanguard spends too much force, assumes armor will carry mistakes, and tries to reset fights instead of exploiting the damaged state already present.

Late Vanguard:
- reads footing earlier
- preserves contact
- converts weapon categories fluidly
- uses walls and enemy bodies
- spends less force for more state change
- recognizes when not to brace
- leaves encounters with less repair debt

## The Inner Heart as Country

The Inner Heart already contains politics, trade, infrastructure, memory, repair, archives, policing, transport, food, and competing claims.

Use existing factions directly.

### Chorus

In this campaign Chorus is a foreign mission power already operating inside the country, not Vanguard's handler.

Function for Vanguard:
- evidence that another external institution is mapping the Heart
- source of restricted routes, abandoned equipment, field packets, and politically loaded safe lanes
- possible buyer of information
- possible source of bureaucratic interference
- proof that someone else treats living territory as data

Vanguard should encounter Chorus indirectly before any direct relationship.

### The Organism

Not a single villain.

It learns through repeated behavior and infrastructure.

Vanguard should discover that the body-country is capable of:
- pricing
- classification
- route recognition
- tolerance
- retaliation
- adaptation
- negotiation through organs and intermediaries

### Ledger Covenant

This is the cleanest bridge from Layowich's social/economic logic.

Existing elements:
- Merchant
- toll mouths
- larders
- scales
- dock mouths
- receipt blisters
- Merchant Claim

Vanguard's lesson:
**payment creates legibility, and legibility creates leverage.**

Paying is not automatically submission.
Refusing is not automatically freedom.

The system remembers both.

### Womb Syndics

Food, healing, breeding, larvae, ferries, and labor animals belong to one economy.

This faction makes bodily need political.

Vanguard needs them because:
- she must eat
- she may need transport
- she may need wound treatment
- they can repair living materials conventional smithing cannot

She distrusts them because:
- they treat tissue as negotiable stock
- healing can create ownership marks
- larvae can become credit

### Maintenance Orders

Their rites began as operational safety and hardened into inherited procedure.

Vanguard responds well to the practical layer:
- tool sequence
- pressure check
- repair timing
- correct hand placement

She becomes hostile when procedure tries to convert her into the missing component.

This is a major origin-era temptation because she respects systems that work.

### Pale Survey

A failed earlier expedition has left route competence embedded in the country.

This is where Vanguard learns from absent better fighters and explorers.

Pell's route discipline should function like the Layowich public duel:
Vanguard sees efficient movement before she can fully reproduce it.

### Archive Rot

Knowledge is useful enough to risk contamination.

Do not make it a lore library.

Every record should:
- solve or sharpen a practical problem
- reveal a useful route, weakness, timing rule, price, or repair method
- make Vanguard more readable in return

### Opened Hunt

Hunters are answers to behavior.

The most important rule:
**a hunter should reveal what habit Vanguard has taught the Heart.**

## Social Areas

The campaign needs settlements, not safe rooms.

They do not need to be human.

Each social zone must answer practical questions:
- what Vanguard can eat
- what locals eat
- what counts as money
- what marks legal identity
- who controls sleeping space
- what privacy means
- who repairs gear
- how parasites / blood / scent are cleaned off
- what local rumor is useful
- what monopoly everyone resents
- what action changes Vanguard from stranger to known quantity

### Settlement 1: The Ration Court

Base canon:
Biomass Larder + Quartermaster of Teeth + Womb Syndics + Ledger Covenant.

Function:
first true settlement.

It is built around food allocation, ration pockets, tooth-counts, larval labor, scales, and sleeping recesses warmed by circulating nutrient channels.

Vanguard arrives hungry enough that the economy matters immediately.

Services:
- food
- boiled / sealed outsider-safe ration
- water-equivalent fluid
- sleeping niche
- simple armor rinse
- local gossip
- route receipts
- minor repairs using platelet resin

Local identity token:
**tooth-count scar** stamped into a removable resin cuff, not her skin at first.

What it does:
- proves she paid for food
- permits one night of sleep
- makes toll mouths treat her as contract-capable
- tells predators and collectors she is inside the Ledger system

Scene chain:
1. classification at the scale
2. Vanguard asks price before touching food
3. first meal
4. Quartermaster notices she pays exact weight rather than overfeeding the system
5. local worker explains which routes are expensive today
6. Vanguard earns a sleeping recess
7. she removes armor and discovers what the last corridor actually did to it
8. first concrete maintenance scene

This is the campaign's Layowich gate + flatbread + Copper Kettle compression.

### Settlement 2: The Scar Exchange

Base canon:
scar mites + route maps + Archive Rot + Pale Survey.

Function:
market for route information, scars, map tissue, broken operator tags, scent masks, recovered tools.

This is not a magic bazaar.
Everything sold must answer a real survival bottleneck.

Trade goods:
- dried route membrane
- scent-scrub paste
- scar-map strips
- preserved operator fasteners
- clean water bladders
- shell oil
- bite wedges
- old Chorus battery cells
- ferryman tokens
- repair larvae

Vanguard can make money here by selling:
- intact hunter plates
- route evidence
- operator hardware
- excess ammunition components
- high-value biological defects

This is where she first realizes information can be more valuable than biomass.

### Settlement 3: The Quiet Berth

Base canon:
Soft Captain + larval ferries + rib vessel locks.

Function:
transport enclave and recovery harbor.

It is socially valuable because it moves people and goods across dangerous body-country.

The Soft Captain is not kind.
The Captain values travelers who:
- do not panic in pressure locks
- protect ferries
- do not overload transport tissue
- understand timing

Vanguard can earn passage through competence rather than payment.

This is a major origin milestone:
someone local recognizes skill before legend.

## Merchant

The Merchant must be an economy before a boss.

Early role:
- useful supplier
- mutation access
- rare repair materials
- ammunition-adjacent biological components
- exact knowledge of scarcity
- remembers deals

Do not make him immediately monstrous in every interaction.

Vanguard should transact with him because he can solve problems no one else can.

His pressure comes from:
- knowing what she will need later
- offering credit when she is materially cornered
- pricing refusal
- knowing prior route behavior
- treating bodily compromise as inventory

He should become personally dangerous only after a long chain of understandable transactions.

The emotional pivot is not "he was evil."

It is:

**Vanguard realizes the same useful accounting system that kept her alive has started counting her as inventory.**

## Heartbeat and Dungeon Pressure

Preserve existing heartbeat / BPM logic.

Campaign use:
heartbeat should become an embodied travel clock.

Low pressure:
- longer social scenes
- maintenance
- haggling
- eating
- route observation
- clean traversal

Rising pressure:
- shorter windows
- transport timing tightens
- hunters spawn closer
- toll mouths close faster
- Merchant prices become worse
- repair quality drops
- locals become less willing to shelter an outsider

Important:
BPM should not only mean "more enemies."

It should compress civil life.

The player should feel the country becoming harder to use.

## Cloning and Hymn Truth

Preserve Inner Heart clone canon.

Vanguard does not replace Hymn's role in that secret.

Use clone infrastructure as evidence of the setting's deeper history:
- repeated body templates
- operator tools sized too consistently
- records that assume recurring entrants
- tissues that identify lineage before identity
- Mitosis Unit technology

Vanguard's campaign may intersect this machinery without solving Hymn's entire mystery.

The practical question for Vanguard:
**what does a society do to identity when bodies can be reproduced, replaced, or recognized by lineage?**

This can support:
- clone ghettos
- escaped body-stock enclaves
- lineage prejudice
- labor castes whose legal identity belongs to a template, not an individual
- black markets in clean identity marks

Do not reveal the full Hymn/Chorus truth unless the campaign specifically earns that later.

## Combat Doctrine

Every major fight follows Vanguard of Glory causal combat.

Required state:
- footing
- weapon ownership
- guard / exposed line
- armor integrity
- enemy structural damage
- wall contact
- prone / stagger
- range
- recoil debt
- local hazard

Major Vanguard verbs:
- shoulder check
- hook
- rake
- brace
- wedge
- trap
- drag
- shove
- short blast
- point-blank blast
- butt strike
- off-hand parry
- off-hand grab
- knee
- stomp

Combat structure:
1. establish material state
2. force commitment
3. change one structural fact
4. carry that fact forward
5. make the next move possible because of it

Examples:
- shoulder charge -> enemy foot leaves safe plate -> wall impact -> shell crack -> bayonet point enters crack
- bayonet hook -> arm trapped -> pull changes facing -> muzzle line opens -> blast -> recoil puts Vanguard near hazard
- off-hand grab -> enemy cannot reset -> knee folds stance -> butt strike drives body into toll teeth
- blast at bad angle -> recoil loses footing -> armor saves injury but Vanguard drops weapon -> recovery becomes next problem

## Learning Fights

Young Vanguard must repeatedly see someone solve violence more economically.

### Fight Lesson 1: Ferry Guard

A nonhuman local uses yielding instead of bracing against a charging predator.

Vanguard initially thinks it surrendered ground.
It actually converts the predator's force into a rib-wall collision.

Lesson:
bracing is not always strength.

### Fight Lesson 2: Pale Survey Houndkeeper

A survivor or inherited route protocol defeats pursuit through foot placement and false gait rather than speed.

Lesson:
movement can attack prediction.

### Fight Lesson 3: Womb Syndic Cutter

A worker uses a short hooked tool to control several limbs by changing one joint angle.

Lesson:
control can be cheaper than damage.

### Fight Lesson 4: Soft Captain Transit Duel

A route enforcer wins by refusing to fight at the opponent's preferred pressure interval.

Lesson:
timing can be terrain.

Vanguard should steal these lessons with her eyes and later reproduce them imperfectly.

## Campaign Acts

### ACT I — Stranger Weight

Purpose:
make Vanguard legible to the country.

Major beats:
1. entry through rib pressure route
2. first toll / classification
3. first hunger problem
4. Ration Court arrival
5. first proper meal
6. first sleep / armor maintenance
7. first local rumor
8. first witnessed fight
9. first Merchant transaction

Boss:
**Blood Ledger juvenile / local collector**

Not a giant climax.
A consequence fight caused by how Vanguard handled early debt / refusal.

Character result:
she learns that every practical shortcut becomes social information.

### ACT II — Useful Stranger

Purpose:
Vanguard acquires local reputation.

Major beats:
1. Scar Exchange
2. route job for payment
3. Pale Survey lesson
4. repair problem forces Maintenance Orders contact
5. Merchant credit becomes genuinely attractive
6. Vanguard protects or abandons transport animal
7. Quartermaster reacts to her account history
8. first mutation or body-altering offer with real utility
9. local NPCs begin recognizing her by conduct

Boss:
**Plate Warden / route champion**

The fight should test overcommitment.

Vanguard wins, but repairs afterward are expensive enough that victory feels materially incomplete.

### ACT III — The Country Notices

Purpose:
the Inner Heart adapts to Vanguard specifically.

Major beats:
1. route counters begin appearing
2. hunter form answers her favored tactic
3. prices shift
4. Chorus infrastructure intersects her route
5. Soft Captain offers politically costly passage
6. clone / lineage social issue becomes visible
7. Merchant starts pricing future behavior rather than goods
8. Vanguard becomes a known category in multiple settlements

Boss:
**Hunter adapted to shoulder-charge / brace habits**

Required lesson:
the thing is built to punish the Vanguard she has been so far.

Victory requires using a lesson learned from another fighter, not more force.

### ACT IV — Who Owns the Body

Purpose:
economic and identity conflict converges.

Major beats:
1. Merchant / Quartermaster / Womb Syndics dispute debt
2. Maintenance Orders try to install Vanguard as a functional component
3. lineage marks affect who treats her as person vs stock
4. social zones choose whether to shelter her based on prior conduct
5. a route closes because she has become too legible
6. she must decide which obligations she honors

Boss candidate:
**Merchant Reckoning**

Only use if earned by long transaction history.

Combat should physically express accounting:
- scales change footing
- tools seize gear
- egg sacs produce replacement limbs / debt collectors
- prior purchases become known vulnerabilities
- Merchant uses the exact categories he sold her

The fight cannot be solved by raw DPS.
Vanguard must break the ledger's control over the arena.

### ACT V — Vanguard

Purpose:
origin transformation.

The final act is not "she becomes strongest."

She becomes the person later stories inherit because she now understands:
- force debt
- social debt
- repair debt
- institutional predation
- practical hospitality
- the value of local knowledge
- how to stay herself without pretending she owes nobody

Possible final structures:
- escape through Soft Captain route
- defeat Merchant and reassign debt infrastructure
- preserve settlements while cutting one predatory covenant
- refuse Chorus extraction bargain
- establish a new traveler category recognized by multiple enclaves

Final boss should depend on route:
- Merchant, if economy dominates
- adaptive Heart champion, if combat pattern dominates
- Maintenance installation body, if procedure dominates
- Chorus extraction machine / proxy, if authority dominates

No single ending should imply every part of the Inner Heart was evil or saved.

## Scene Rhythm

Use the Vanguard of Glory cadence:

social transaction
-> preparation
-> local threat
-> consequence
-> recovery
-> investigation
-> combat

Repeat with changing scale.

Do not stack three boss fights without a meal, repair bill, bed, argument, route purchase, or practical job between them.

## Recovery Doctrine

Do not skip the bath.

Campaign equivalents:
- wash blood scent off armor
- remove parasite eggs from seams
- dry scattergun internals
- clean bayonet points
- replace resin packing
- repair bent plate
- eat enough protein / ration mass to restore function
- sleep somewhere that does not identify her as food
- negotiate privacy
- decide which damage cannot be fixed yet

Recovery scenes should expose:
- costs
- local labor
- intimacy
- embarrassment
- rumors
- future vulnerabilities

## Progression

Progression is not just mutation gain.

Track:
- movement lessons
- social marks
- faction trust
- known routes
- weapon repairs
- armor state
- ammunition economy
- Merchant history
- local recipes / safe foods
- scar-map knowledge
- fighting reputation
- learned tactical conversions

A major progression reward should usually solve one bottleneck and create another.

## Character Arc

Beginning:
"I can force my way through if I have to."

Middle:
"I can force this, but then I own the repair, the noise, the price, and whatever learned from it."

End:
"I know what this move buys before I spend it."

That is the origin-era transformation.

## First Playable Campaign Slice

Build the first slice from existing Inner Heart content rather than inventing a vertical slice from scratch.

### Sequence

1. **Rib Vessel Lock**
   - Vanguard enters alone.
   - She can sync, pay, or force.
   - Force should visibly damage gear / route tissue.
   - Payment creates first ledger recognition.

2. **Biomass Larder / Ration Court threshold**
   - hunger matters immediately
   - Quartermaster system distinguishes taking, paying, and leaving hungry
   - give Vanguard one pleasurable food beat, not only horror

3. **Ration Court**
   - registration-equivalent resin cuff
   - lodging
   - armor rinse
   - route gossip
   - first minor merchant economy

4. **Public local fight**
   - Vanguard watches an economical local combatant
   - no exposition lecture
   - later fight should punish Vanguard for not yet embodying the lesson

5. **Merchant arrival**
   - offer something she truly needs for scattergun / armor upkeep
   - she bargains
   - price should be concrete
   - no boss framing yet

6. **First job**
   - escort ration tissue / ferries through a route the Opened Hunt is disrupting
   - social obligation turns into combat

7. **First campaign fight**
   - Vanguard overcommits
   - armor prevents catastrophe
   - weapon position / footing debt persists
   - she wins by exploiting a defect created earlier in the exchange

8. **Return to Ration Court**
   - repair bill
   - locals now know what she did
   - one person treats her differently
   - Quartermaster updates her category
   - Merchant price changes based on observed need

### Slice boss

Blood Ledger collector.

State-driven gimmick:
it carries resin from her unpaid / paid / forced route history and fights differently based on that history.

The boss is therefore not a random gate.
It is the country's first invoice.

## Acceptance Criteria

The campaign is on-model when:

- Vanguard is alone by default.
- The later crew is absent.
- Inner Heart factions remain recognizable and functional.
- At least three social zones work as actual settlements.
- Food, sleep, hygiene, gear repair, trade, rank/identity, and local rumor matter.
- Merchant is useful for hours before any boss treatment.
- Combat state persists.
- Major finishers emerge from structural openings.
- Vanguard makes at least one expensive overcommitment mistake.
- She learns visibly from a better fighter.
- BPM compresses social/economic life, not only combat.
- Clone infrastructure remains Inner Heart canon and is not casually overwritten.
- A faction can help Vanguard while still charging a cost.
- A faction can oppose Vanguard without being generically evil.
- Every act includes recovery and practical logistics.
- The ending follows repeated conduct, not a morality meter.
- Prose remains concrete, operational, and causal.
