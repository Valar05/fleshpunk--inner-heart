## Vanguard Combat-Cultivation System

The campaign's combat doctrine must use the full first-party Vanguard loop, not only melee state transitions.

### Core loop

**LIVE -> ENCOUNTER -> MODEL -> FIGHT -> CONSEQUENCE -> RECOVER -> REFLECT -> BIND -> EXPERIMENT -> INTEGRATE**

Around the whole loop:

**ECONOMY / SOCIETY / ECOLOGY**

This means a fight does not end when the opponent falls. It ends when the campaign knows what Vanguard spent, broke, learned, harvested, repaired, carried, sold, owed, or permanently incorporated.

### Combat stack

Every major fight may draw from these layers:

**World / motive**
- why the fight is happening
- faction, ecology, job, road pressure, debt, local law, reputation
- what changes outside the arena

**Progression**
- rank
- Cladding
- Essence Binding / Tempering analogues or campaign equivalents
- residual body development
- permanent versus consumable growth resources
- learned technique

**Resource economy**
- personal reserve
- ambient draw
- attuned equipment
- battery / external supply
- potions
- ammunition
- consumable growth material
- hunger, water, fatigue

**Perception**
- normal senses
- enhanced senses
- aura-like / biological read modes
- hidden actors
- rank / charge / dependency cues
- opponent habits
- terrain readability

**Material**
- armor response time
- weapon material
- charge capacity
- structural damage
- repair burden
- carry capacity

**Technique**
- stance
- guard
- grip
- footwork
- compact scatter form
- extended halberd form
- transformation under contact
- off-hand control
- air / terrain manipulation when available

**Immediate state**
- range
- footing
- facing
- balance
- contact
- weapon ownership
- armor integrity
- structural openings
- wall contact
- recoil / recovery debt

**Aftermath**
- loot / harvest
- food
- cleaning
- repair
- social reaction
- reputation
- advancement gained or delayed
- emotional residue

The halberd / scatter melee ontology is one vocabulary inside this architecture.

### Vanguard reasoning loop

Write and design her fights around:

**OBSERVE -> INFER -> TEST -> EXPLOIT -> PAY COST -> UPDATE MODEL**

Vanguard should win by noticing concrete facts:
- what is infused / protected and what is not
- what is feeding an opponent's throughput
- what limb, seam, plate, tendon, grip, route, or habit is structurally important
- what terrain can be manipulated
- what the opponent cannot redirect once committed
- what dependency can be removed
- what earlier damage can be reused

Do not replace this with generic attack escalation.

### Progression as lived discovery

New combat capability should not arrive fully understood.

Use:

**experience -> concept -> meditation / reflection -> partial acquisition -> experimentation -> tactical incorporation -> identity**

A new capability should normally produce:
1. an obvious hypothesis
2. at least one failed or limited experiment
3. a reliable practical use
4. a surprising second use later
5. a new cost, appetite, repair need, dependency, or recognition problem

This applies especially well to Inner Heart mutations, symbiotes, living gear, and the new transformable weapon.

### Future-self cost

Emergency combat choices may consume resources intended for advancement.

The campaign should preserve the first-party principle that survival can cost future growth:
- spend rare biological material now instead of integrating it later
- burn a mutation seed before stabilization
- overuse a symbiote and lose a future bond path
- consume repair stock meant for weapon development
- accept Merchant credit that solves the current fight but narrows later autonomy

A combat resource can therefore represent **future Vanguard**, not only current stamina.

### Material law

Power must change handling.

For the new weapon system:

**Extended form**
- owns range
- creates line control
- supports thrust, reap, sweep, hook, brace, drag
- higher leverage and commitment
- greater recovery / redirection debt

**Compact scatter form**
- close control
- short leverage
- trapping
- butt strikes
- point-blank blast
- rapid recovery under pressure

**Transformation**
- not a neutral stance menu
- a tactical verb
- collapse under bind
- extend while dragging
- retract after a miss
- fire during hook / control
- shorten grip when crowded
- use transformation to change what the next move can be

Mass / Sharpness / Wedge logic remains the conceptual model:
- Mass = inertia, commitment, impact, anchoring, handling debt
- Sharpness = force concentration through an interface
- Wedge = pressure / momentum concentrated through an opening

### Terrain is inventory

The original Vanguard fights treat the environment as usable equipment.

Campaign fights should look for:
- loose stones
- ribs / walls
- soft floors
- pressure doors
- corpses
- enemy weapons
- scales
- larvae
- repair animals
- hanging tissue
- fluid
- narrow lanes
- scent routes
- exposed supports

If the environment could be removed without changing the fight, reconsider whether the scene is exploiting Inner Heart enough.

### Recovery closes the loop

After major violence, show the bill.

Possible recovery beats:
- wash scent / blood / spores away
- dry scattergun internals
- clean or replace weapon packing
- repair bent armor
- remove eggs / parasites
- eat enough to restore metabolic loss
- drink after integration strain
- sleep somewhere socially safe
- pay labor
- discover damage that was hidden by armor during the fight
- decide what cannot be fixed tonight

Do not skip the bath remains literal campaign doctrine.

### Reputation is infrastructure

Combat reputation should change services and treatment rather than only increase a number.

Possible consequences:
- cheaper or more expensive lodging
- free meal
- repair priority
- route favor
- higher toll
- Merchant price shift
- children / workers asking for the story
- a faction recognizing Vanguard's technique
- a settlement deciding she is worth sheltering
- a local fighter challenging or teaching her
- a predator being prepared specifically for her

### Writing rule

For every meaningful combat exchange, ask:

1. What tactical fact changed?
2. What broader cost or opportunity changed?

Examples:
- a parry changes line and drains stored attunement
- a Mass-like strike breaks stance and spends future growth material
- a read mode reveals a dependency but obscures terrain
- a potion restores reserves but consumes prior preparation
- a structural wound creates the next attack route and the later repair bill
- victory produces harvest and a carrying-capacity problem
- a witnessed technique changes local reputation

### Campaign character arc through combat

Early Vanguard:
- can force solutions
- spends too much
- treats armor as enough margin
- knows many techniques but not yet their full economy

Middle Vanguard:
- sees dependencies earlier
- turns observation into cheaper control
- experiments with new weapon and body capabilities
- begins choosing what future resource is worth spending now

Late Vanguard:
- understands what a move buys before spending it
- transforms weapon form according to state rather than spectacle
- uses less force to produce more structural change
- carries fight consequences into social, economic, and progression choices consciously

This is the combat system the surprise-writing campaign should write through.
