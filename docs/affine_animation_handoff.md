# Affine Animation Handoff

This is the handoff prompt for Fleshpunk Inner Heart. Use the phrase `affine animation` as the key term. The deliverable is a large prompt that explains the repo and the motion language, not a code implementation.

## What This Repo Is

`fleshpunk--inner-heart` is a Godot 4.5 mobile text roguelike. The runtime is text-first, with room descriptions, event narration, pulse timing, story follow-ups, and a full-screen text console. Animation here is not about sprites or camera tricks. Animation is about pulse, rhythm, fade, pressure, and the way the room seems to breathe.

## What Affine Animation Means Here

Affine animation means the visible and textual motion should stay coherent under timing changes, line breaks, fades, and pulse transitions. The motion should feel like a living chamber that inhales and exhales without breaking the scene grammar.

Use it for:

- room fade in and fade out
- pulse events and heart timing
- breathing walls and moving seams
- slow pressure rise and relief
- button emphasis and text emphasis
- follow-up beats that arrive after a delay

## Belly Example

Use `slime sine cosine breathing animation in belly` as the canonical example of the timing style. In this repo that means:

- a chamber or wall swells slowly on a sine rise
- the swell rounds off on a cosine peak
- the release is slower than a snap and faster than a collapse
- the text, pulse, and room fade stay in phase
- the effect reads as body pressure, not decorative wobble

The point is not literal slime. The point is rhythmic body pressure.

## Motion And Narrative Contract

The prompt should explain that the animation is part of the narrative pressure system:

- the room can pulse before a choice appears
- the text can arrive with a heartbeat delay
- the scene can breathe to show danger or calm
- a follow-up can feel like a late pulse rather than an instant reward
- the room should remain readable even when the pressure rises

Keep the motion disciplined. Every pulse should mean something.

## What The Next Operator Should Say

The next operator should describe:

1. the room as a living chamber
2. the pulse as the timing rail
3. the text as the visible result of pressure
4. the breathing or swelling body as the organic motif
5. the follow-up as delayed consequence
6. the fade and transition as affine motion, not cinematic fluff

## Hard Noes

- no literal 3D
- no generic animation jargon without a scene anchor
- no sprite-heavy rewrite
- no decorative motion that has no narrative function
- no implementation work when the request is only for a handoff prompt

## Deliverable Shape

The export should be a repo-aware prompt that tells a future animator or prompt writer how to express Fleshpunk Inner Heart as pulse-driven affine animation. It should be explicit about breathing, timing, and pressure while staying faithful to the text-first nature of the project.
