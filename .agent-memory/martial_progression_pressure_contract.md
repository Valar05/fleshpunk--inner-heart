# Martial Progression Pressure Contract

Fleshpunk is now a martial progression fantasy first.

The player loop is:

1. A room pressures Hymn's current body.
2. Hymn answers with baseline skill, mutation, symbiote, avoidance, bargain, or cost.
3. The run records what kind of fighter she is becoming.
4. Later rooms, factions, predators, and mutation offers react to that record.

Combat is at the forefront, but combat should often be avoidable. Avoidance is still a martial choice: restraint, timing, intimidation, route knowledge, bargaining from strength, or refusing a bad exchange.

Rooms must not be puzzles. A room should not ask for a correct input sequence. It should ask what kind of body, fighter, predator, witness, or survivor Hymn chooses to be under pressure.

## Forward Pressure Axes

### Hunt Pressure

Use when Hymn becomes easier to track or worth hunting.

Raised by:

- loud kills
- broken routes
- blood scent
- retreating from unresolved predators
- repeated force through living infrastructure
- leaving wounded enemies alive and informed

Consequences:

- pursuit events
- ambush predators
- enemies that start closer
- hunters adapted to her last tactics
- route text that mentions scent, vibration, blood warmth, or listening tissue

Corpus pressure:

- pulp pursuit
- hostile landscape as actor
- predator identity

### Body Drift

Use when Hymn solves problems by becoming less baseline and more organism, graft, predator, or tool.

Raised by:

- taking mutations
- buying mutation eggs
- accepting symbiote dependency
- letting repair organisms revise her body
- using biological shortcuts that rewrite movement or appetite

Consequences:

- stronger body-specific options
- rooms misclassify Hymn as organ, predator, debt object, operator, or breeding surface
- factions react to visible drift
- mutation appetite and social/ecological recognition deepen

This replaces generic corruption as the forward creative frame. Existing `corruption` can remain as a compatibility counter, but new writing should think in body drift.

### Baseline Discipline

Use when Hymn refuses body rewriting and wins or survives by clean movement, timing, breath, pain tolerance, and tactical reading.

Raised by:

- winning or escaping without mutations
- refusing grafts and easy biological shortcuts
- reading a fight before committing
- clean retreats that preserve control
- keeping body boundaries under pressure

Consequences:

- stance, breath, and recovery options
- purist witness/faction recognition
- cleaner route trust
- better options to read enemy posture
- fewer mutation shortcuts

Baseline is not "no progression." It is a pure-body progression path.

### Wound Debt

Use when Hymn survives by taking damage, overcommitting, forcing contact, or letting a room mark her body.

Raised by:

- bracing through strikes
- forced entries
- overusing heavy mutations
- fighting while already hurt
- allowing repairs that leave exploitable seams

Consequences:

- later rooms notice scars, split surfaces, damaged shoulders, torn tails, breath debt, or tender joints
- enemies target marked body parts
- repair offers become more tempting
- some factions respect survival marks

### Recognition

Use when Hymn's conduct changes how others classify her.

Raised by:

- sparing, killing, or humiliating rivals
- refusing grafts in front of witnesses
- fighting cleanly or brutally
- repeatedly paying or refusing tolls
- using a visible mutation style
- carrying scars from known predator classes

Consequences:

- faction respect, fear, suspicion, debt, or rivalry
- altered dialogue
- different bargaining terms
- social escalation toward endings

### Route Memory

Use when environment families learn Hymn's repeated tactics.

Raised by:

- repeating a method in similar rooms
- always breaking locks
- always taking quiet routes
- always retreating from a predator family
- repeatedly using the same mutation to solve a route problem

Consequences:

- later room text shifts
- room variants adapt
- shortcuts open
- old tactics become less safe
- environment-specific endings become available

## Required Scenario Metadata

Every forward scenario should declare:

- `primary_pressure`: the main pressure axis the room applies.
- `body_path_pressure`: how the room pressures mutation, symbiote, or baseline discipline.
- `avoidance_route`: how combat can be avoided, refused, postponed, bargained around, or converted into another cost.
- `recognition_effect`: who or what learns something about Hymn from this choice.

These fields are designer metadata. They should not be visible risk labels in player-facing prose.

## Room Type Brainstorm

Combat-forward, avoidable:

- `ambush_body`: predator is already in reach; fight, misdirect, retreat, or feed it another target.
- `duel_gate`: rival blocks a route; defeat, impress, bargain, yield, or learn from them.
- `pursuit_lane`: something follows; choose speed, silence, confrontation, terrain abuse, or bait.
- `clinch_room`: cramped space where tails, blades, shells, and extra limbs change what is possible.
- `open_killing_floor`: reach, mobility, and line control matter; avoidance costs route position.
- `wounded_predator`: kill, spare, harvest, follow, heal, or turn it into a future rival.

Interpersonal:

- `mutation_surgeon`: offers body change with philosophy, appetite, and social cost.
- `rival_disciple`: another fighter recognizes Hymn's path and tests it.
- `purist_witness`: rewards baseline victories and distrusts grafted shortcuts.
- `symbiote_negotiation`: dependency, timing, hunger, and consent under pressure.
- `faction_toll`: pay with biomass, scar, proof of combat, silence, or refusal.

Noncombat, not puzzles:

- `hostile_crossing`: terrain pressures movement; mutations change traversal and social interpretation.
- `rest_with_teeth`: recovery scene with vulnerability, witness, debt, or changed scent.
- `molting_threshold`: temporary weakness for permanent body reconfiguration.
- `feeding_site`: biomass temptation that attracts attention or alters appetite.
- `training_echo`: a previous fight pattern returns and can be refined or rejected.
- `body_shrine`: a place that recognizes what Hymn has become, not a riddle to solve.

## Corpus Inspiration

Use corpus to inspire pressure, not surface style:

- pulp pursuit -> hunt pressure and route urgency
- duel/reversal -> opponent reads Hymn and changes position
- hostile landscape -> route memory and environmental pressure
- lost-world threshold -> social/ecological recognition
- martial research -> range, line, tempo, leverage, recovery
- biology research -> mutation strengths, weaknesses, appetite, and environmental unlocks
- progression fantasy -> identity narrowing, breakthrough, rank pressure, and long-term recognition
