# Fleshpunk Research: Biology And Mutation

## Scope

This note turns biological references into original mutation design. It is taxonomy and system guidance only.

Core premise: anatomy is an answer to a tactical or environmental problem.

## Design Principle

Do not generate "body horror mutation." Generate "adaptation under pressure."

Every mutation should answer:

- What problem does this solve?
- What movement does it improve?
- What movement does it ruin?
- What range does it prefer?
- What enemy behavior does it punish?
- What enemy behavior punishes it?
- What environmental pressure selected for it?
- What future room verb does it unlock?

## Arthropod Layer

Research pattern: exoskeleton, segmentation, jointed appendages, molt vulnerability, limb specialization.

Fleshpunk transformations:

- segmented armor should create directional protection and joint gaps
- molting should be a progression event with temporary weakness and permanent body reconfiguration
- specialized limbs should replace general capability with a precise tactical role
- detachable limbs or sacrificial plates can become emergency choices
- chitin, calcified plate, and flexible membrane should have different noise, heat, healing, and mobility profiles

Mutation examples:

- `rib carapace`: frontal protection, poor twist, heat buildup
- `molting scar`: one-time vulnerability, then new stance or armor shape
- `hinged heel-spur`: fast backward kick, poor silent movement
- `mantle plates`: protects organs, catches in narrow ducts
- `joint gap sense`: predicts where armored enemies are weak

## Cephalopod Layer

Research pattern: flexible limbs, distributed control, suckers, camouflage, texture shifts, jet movement, ink/decoy escape.

Fleshpunk transformations:

- distributed limbs can act as semi-autonomous guards, probes, or grapples
- sucker arrays should control surface, texture, weight, and local leverage
- camouflage should depend on texture, stillness, surface reading, and stress
- ink analogues should be decoys, scent clouds, clot bursts, or memory smoke
- soft-body movement should improve entry/escape while worsening impact resistance

Mutation examples:

- `sucker braid`: controls two surfaces, weak to tearing
- `skin script`: mimics wall texture, fails under bright heat or open flame
- `ink clot`: leaves a false pulse trail and breaks pursuit
- `distributed grip`: can hold a latch while the main body fights
- `funnel lung`: burst movement with breath debt

## Predatory Insect Layer

Research pattern: sit-and-wait predation, raptorial limbs, exact strike pocket, visual timing, prey-speed adjustment.

Fleshpunk transformations:

- ambush mutations should reward patience and punish panic
- raptorial limbs should be strongest inside a narrow measure band
- strike timing can be tuned to enemy speed, but over-tuning creates vulnerability to feints
- sensory organs should read approach speed, line, vibration, or heat rather than grant generic detection

Mutation examples:

- `raptorial brace`: catches enemies that enter the pocket, poor pursuit
- `compound blink`: reads movement bursts, weak in smoke or stillness
- `strike tendon`: one explosive capture, then tremor/recovery
- `prey-speed nerve`: better counters against fast enemies, vulnerable to slow feints

## Deep-Sea Layer

Research pattern: pressure, darkness, cold, scarce food, bioluminescence, counterillumination, lures, vertical migration, marine snow.

Fleshpunk transformations:

- pressure adaptation becomes tolerance for compression, crushing corridors, vacuum of breath, or deep-organ stress
- darkness adaptation becomes nonvisual sense, lure detection, or light economy
- food scarcity becomes opportunistic metabolism and delayed digestion
- bioluminescence becomes lure, decoy, social signal, threat flash, or camouflage against a bright background
- marine-snow logic becomes environments fed by falling waste, dead tissue, spores, or broken pilgrims

Mutation examples:

- `lure node`: attracts small prey or attention; dangerous when predators answer
- `pressure lung`: survives crushing rooms, weak at surface heat
- `cold blood reserve`: slows resource burn, slows recovery
- `counterlight skin`: hides from below/behind certain light fields
- `detritus mouth`: feeds from room waste, raises contamination pressure

## Anti-Patterns

- Random limb added for visual shock.
- Mutation with only stat bonuses.
- No downside, appetite, social cost, or movement change.
- Biology term used as flavor without a mechanical behavior.
- Camouflage that works everywhere.
- Armor with no heat, molt, joint, stealth, or flexibility cost.
- Venom or acid that does not change enemy behavior, space, or future risk.
