# Fleshpunk: Inner Heart - Lore Guide

This guide defines what is true, what Hymn can know, who matters, and how lore becomes gameplay.

The vibe guide controls tone.
This guide controls continuity.
The setting backbone controls faction, character, ecology, and cross-run story architecture.

Use `.agent-memory/setting_backbone.md` before generating new rooms. New room content should express a story already happening in the organism through faction behavior, animal infrastructure, recurring character traces, room memory, and delayed consequences.

## Core Premise

Hymn enters a buried organic facility under Chorus direction.

The facility is not abandoned. It is a living system with organs, routes, immune responses, exchange mechanisms, parasites, and memory-like residue.

The organism does not merely contain encounters.
It studies repeated behavior and pushes Hymn toward an outcome.

## Knowledge Layers

### Hymn Can Know

- She is on mission under Chorus authority.
- Chorus can issue orders, request reports, and approve route decisions.
- The facility is organic, old, and deliberately structured.
- The organism reacts to repeated behavior.
- Mutations, symbiotes, biomass, danger, and corruption are field realities.
- Some memories feel misplaced or too familiar.

### Hymn Can Suspect

- Chorus withholds context.
- The facility has been entered before.
- The merchant knows more than a chance encounter should.
- The organism recognizes her body faster than it should.
- Some rooms feel like they were waiting for a specific pattern.

### Hymn Cannot Know

- She is a clone.
- Runs are repeated clone instances.
- Chorus may be using repeated Hymn bodies as probes.
- Prior-run memory bleed is structurally real.
- The merchant may be exploiting the clone cycle.

### Behind The Curtain

- Every run is a new Hymn instance.
- Some memory persists imperfectly.
- Chorus knows more than Hymn does.
- The organism learns from pattern, not morality.
- The merchant is not a normal person. He is an exchange intelligence with long-term leverage.

## Factions

### Chorus

Remote command, mission authority, and filter.

Visible role:
- gives orders offscreen
- receives field reports
- approves dangerous acquisition choices
- may go quiet under pressure

Hidden role:
- may know Hymn's repeated nature
- may be studying the organism through her
- may prioritize data over survival

Gameplay hooks:
- Chorus report events
- signal degradation at high corruption
- route authorization choices
- lore fragments that ask whether Chorus omitted context

### The Organism

The living facility, dungeon, ecology, and pressure director.

Visible role:
- reacts to danger and corruption
- grows rooms, seals routes, releases hunters
- prices repetition through pressure

Hidden role:
- learns usable patterns across runs
- distinguishes appetite, fear, dependence, violence, and restraint
- may be growing a negotiation interface through the merchant

Gameplay hooks:
- director warnings
- deck composition changes
- pressure-axis endings
- rooms that mutate after repeated choices

### The Merchant

A predatory exchange system with a body.

Visible role:
- appears at intervals or from impossible side routes
- trades biomass for mutations
- remembers refusal
- turns repeated avoidance into reckoning

Hidden role:
- may be the organism's economic organ
- may be testing what Hymn will pay to remain herself
- may be accumulating claim over future bodies

Gameplay hooks:
- mutation shop
- debt and refusal pressure
- corruption temptation
- merchant reckoning ending

### Facility Operators / Cult

The human or post-human group that built, worshipped, studied, or fed the organism.

Visible role:
- dead notes
- ritualized tools
- observation stations
- warning fragments

Hidden role:
- may have confused worship with containment
- may have engineered parts of the exchange system
- may have seeded symbiotes as operational gear

Gameplay hooks:
- lore fragments with secondary effects
- old protocols that lower danger or raise corruption
- room tags unlocked by reading facility records

### Immune Beasts / Hunters

The organism's pursuit logic made physical.

Visible role:
- blood hunters and stalkers respond to noise, scent, and avoidance
- danger makes them more frequent and larger

Hidden role:
- not random monsters
- each hunter is an answer to a player pattern

Gameplay hooks:
- danger and avoidance thresholds
- forced hunter reckoning
- enemy tiers and deck pressure

### Symbiotes

Living tools that survive through partnership.

Visible role:
- manually activated tactical aid
- damaged or lost through use
- bonding kills unchosen symbiotes

Hidden role:
- may carry old operator design logic
- may recognize repeated Hymn bodies before Hymn does
- create dependence pressure

Gameplay hooks:
- active-use choices in rooms
- dependence warnings
- symbiote-specific lore fragments
- mutation/symbiote interaction branches

### Mutations

Purchasable body edits offered by the merchant.

Visible role:
- power with immediate tradeoff
- corruption pressure

Hidden role:
- each mutation teaches the organism what shape Hymn will accept
- repeated purchases make future offers more intimate and invasive

Gameplay hooks:
- corruption ending pressure
- merchant pricing changes
- mutation-specific event options
- altered narration at thresholds

## Recurring Characters

### Hymn

Field operative. First-person narrator. Does not know the clone truth.

Use:
- practical field reports
- bodily observation
- Chorus requests
- flashes of uncertainty

Avoid:
- meta language
- clone awareness
- visible speaker labels such as "Her:"
- third-person narration about Hymn
- heroic speeches
- omniscient lore explanation

### Chorus

Unheard authority.

Use:
- "Chorus, confirm route."
- "Chorus, contact."
- "Chorus, I need instruction."
- "No reply. I proceed."

Avoid:
- printing Chorus dialogue
- making Chorus warm or chatty
- explaining Chorus motives too early

### The Merchant

Recurring antagonist and exchange intelligence.

Use:
- precise transactions
- scales, eggs, tools, silent route changes
- patient consequences

Avoid:
- normal shopkeeper jokes
- friendly bargaining
- exposition dumps

### The Hunter

Consequence made flesh.

Use:
- pursuit signs before arrival
- repeated auditory or scent motifs
- forced reckoning after avoidance

Avoid:
- random jump scare framing
- making it feel unrelated to player choices

## Lore Fragment Rules

Every lore fragment must have:
- safe reveal
- deferred secret
- secondary effect
- gameplay hook

Valid secondary effects:
- lower danger through better route knowledge
- raise danger by waking dormant systems
- raise corruption by letting the organism read Hymn
- unlock a merchant line
- alter a future deck draw
- add or remove an event option
- flag ending eligibility

Lore fragments should be short, damaged, and operational.
They are not codex pages.

## Relationship Web

- Chorus uses Hymn as a probe.
- Hymn trusts Chorus because mission structure is all she has.
- The organism studies Hymn's repeated patterns.
- The merchant prices Hymn's needs and refusals.
- Symbiotes depend on Hymn and may recognize continuity she cannot.
- Mutations make Hymn easier for the organism to understand.
- Hunters punish distance, refusal, and unresolved threat.
- Facility records show prior attempts without explaining the clone cycle directly.

## First-Pass Lore Expansion

Design thesis:
Every new lore element should expose a lever the organism can pull when the player repeats a behavior. Lore becomes route locks, deck shifts, warnings, counters, and ending gravity.

The organism tracks pressure shapes:
- fight
- flee
- harvest
- file reports
- mutate
- consult symbiotes
- obey procedures
- repeat routes

Each pattern should offer a short-term benefit, then a warning, then a counter.

### Operator Remnants

Defunct oversight cells embedded in bone stations.

Safe reveal:
- Hymn finds bone consoles, stamped protocols, lockout tags, and checklists that still trigger doors and suppressors.

Hymn's read:
- Prior facility staff left practical instructions.

Hidden truth:
- Some procedures fit Hymn's body profile too well. They imply prior entries without saying clone.
- Following the protocols teaches the organism restraint patterns.

Gameplay hooks:
- Procedure-active status: lower danger for the next few rooms.
- Repeating procedures raises corruption or pattern lock.
- Obeying three different procedures without harvesting can flag Neutral Access.
- Procedures can unlock better observation options such as `observe_organ_chamber`.

Sample fragment:
- "Bone console still has charge. Three steps: fix breath, mark artery, do not harvest. I follow the list. Doors listen to lists."

### Resin Syndic

Amber toll system and merchant-linked exchange organ.

Safe reveal:
- Amber plaques list tolls: pay biomass for quieter passage, or refuse and carry the noise.

Hymn's read:
- A local toll system the merchant can read.

Hidden truth:
- The Syndic and merchant share ledger state.
- Repeated refusal increases Merchant Claim.

Gameplay hooks:
- `pay_resin_toll`: spend biomass to lower danger for several rooms.
- Refuse multiple tolls: raise Merchant Claim and hunter weight.
- Paying several tolls can discount a future mutation, but make the next offer more intimate and invasive.
- Claim reaching a threshold schedules Merchant Reckoning.

Sample fragment:
- "Amber plaque extrudes a slot the size of my palm. It wants price up front. I can grease the lane or carry the noise."

### Choir Of Baffles

Respiratory routing organs that move air, scent, and sound.

Safe reveal:
- Hymn can turn baffles to mute or amplify flow.

Hymn's read:
- Ventilation controls to avoid pursuit.

Hidden truth:
- Changing baffles is a declaration of intent.
- Repeated muting creates a stealth-reliance pattern.

Gameplay hooks:
- `turn_baffle`: lower danger now.
- Repeated use schedules a Smother Hunter.
- First use can be strong; later uses should become risky.

Sample fragment:
- "Baffles hum. I can choke the lane's breath and bleed my scent sideways. Quiet buys seconds. Sometimes debts."

### Wardens Of Plate

Calcified immune strata that reward walking marked paths and punish predictability.

Safe reveal:
- Marked plate lanes are safer if walked as marked.

Hymn's read:
- Clear route rules.

Hidden truth:
- The plates are measuring obedience.
- Overusing marked lanes lets the Wardens pin Hymn and present her to a hunter gate.

Gameplay hooks:
- Marked route streak: first and second marked lanes lower danger.
- Third consecutive marked lane schedules Plate Snare unless the player breaks pattern.
- Breaking pattern resets the streak and can temporarily reduce hunter weight.

Sample fragment:
- "Plates show a clean line. My feet want it. The route wants me to be predictable."

### Green Archivum

Symbiote seed library embedded in green spines and living glossaries.

Safe reveal:
- Hymn can consult dormant symbiote scripts for tactical notes.

Hymn's read:
- A dangerous but useful field glossary.

Hidden truth:
- Consulting repeatedly bonds micro-filaments.
- Future symbiote hosts become more eager and more costly.

Gameplay hooks:
- `consult_archive`: unlock a stronger future symbiote option.
- Add Dependence Marks.
- Three Dependence Marks trigger a director dependence warning.
- Stronger options should carry lower health, harsher recharge, or sharper corruption pressure.

Sample fragment:
- "Filaments twitch when I read them. The glossaries are alive enough to care that I looked."

### Hemophage Guild

Parasitic maintenance caste that sells speed by drinking timing and pressure.

Safe reveal:
- A leech can be rented for a temporary initiative boost.

Hymn's read:
- A risky tool for speed.

Hidden truth:
- Repeated rentals create a circulatory map the organism uses to pre-aim ambushes.

Gameplay hooks:
- `rent_leech`: gain initiative for the next few fights.
- After a countdown, schedule an ambush or raise danger.
- Multiple rentals increase global hunter weight.

Sample fragment:
- "The leech tests my pulse with tiny teeth. I let it drink a timing window and buy my next step."

### Chorus Field Office

Bone shrines and telemetry sites that let Hymn file reports to unseen authority.

Safe reveal:
- Hymn can file a report to steady herself and request route clearance.

Hymn's read:
- Reports help unlock safer routing.

Hidden truth:
- The walls may hear the report too.
- Frequent filing reduces surprise but lets the organism tailor pressure later.

Gameplay hooks:
- `file_report`: reduce short-term deck volatility.
- Later rooms pre-adapt to the last damage type, route habit, or symbiote use.
- Selective filing can support Neutral Access; spam filing should harm it.

Sample fragment:
- "I send metrics and ask for clearance. The walls click like they heard the report too."

## First-Pass Recurring Figures

### Hunter: Blood Ledger

A named hunter variant that enforces debt and unfinished fights.

Safe reveal:
- Appears after strings of refusals, retreats, and unpaid tolls.

Hymn's read:
- A larger hunter keyed to heat, resin, and route noise.

Hidden truth:
- It follows unpaid exchanges.

Gameplay hooks:
- Spawn from retreat/refusal streaks.
- Killing it can reduce Merchant Claim once per run.
- Fleeing it schedules Merchant Reckoning sooner.

Sample fragment:
- "It wears old resin on its shins like badges. It knows the places I said no."

### Echo Technician

A ghosted operator presence in records and mechanisms.

Safe reveal:
- Short, precise notes improve approach options near organs and cores.

Hymn's read:
- Another field tech walked smart and left useful notes.

Hidden truth:
- The notes fit Hymn's hands and timing too precisely.

Gameplay hooks:
- Echo logs unlock observation variants and reduce ambush risk.
- Repeated use adds corruption or pattern lock.
- Echo notes should imply prior entries without saying clone.

Sample fragment:
- "Her checklist matches my hands without needing edits. That's not normal."

### Spindle-Keeper

An organism sub-mind at split corridors.

Safe reveal:
- Forks feel easier when Hymn keeps making the same kind of choice.

Hymn's read:
- Airflow, instinct, or route sense.

Hidden truth:
- It trains repeated intent into an ending.

Gameplay hooks:
- Repeating path type grants a small immediate boon.
- Gift/recovery repetition pushes corruption.
- Avoidance/red-route repetition pushes danger or hunter pressure.
- Varied routing protects Neutral Access.

Sample fragment:
- "The right-hand lane feels cut for my stride. It would be easy to take it again."

## First-Pass Lore Fragments

Operational tag:
- "Isolate. Vent. Proceed single-file."

Hook:
- Following the steps lowers danger.
- Repeating the ritual too often raises corruption or pattern lock.

Sample:

Amber plaque listing exchange rates.

Hook:
- Unlocks `pay_resin_toll`.
- Pay to lower danger.
- Skip too often to raise Merchant Claim.

Sample:

Field shard about organ timing windows.

Hook:
- Unlocks better `observe_organ_chamber` odds.
- Repeated use teaches the organism Hymn's rhythm.

Sample:
- "Two-count on the downbeat. Cut on the up. I can hear it now."

### Spindle Oath

Ritual line at a split:
- "Repeat the path and be carried."

Hook:
- Repeating a path type grants biomass or safety now.
- Repetition pushes an ending axis.

Sample:

### Leech Rental Stub

Receipt spine for borrowed speed.

Hook:
- Shows a countdown to payback.
- Countdown reaching zero schedules an ambush.
- Multiple stubs increase hunter weight.

Sample:

### Baffle Maplet

Vent routing scrap.

Hook:
- Unlocks `turn_baffle`.

Sample:

## First-Pass Relationship Web

- Hymn files reports to Chorus; the walls may price the report before Chorus can answer.
- Chorus appears clean and procedural; its timing grows suspicious through pre-filed reports and anticipated injuries.
- The organism reacts first to actions, then to repeated verbs, then to predicted choices.
- The merchant and Resin Syndic share a ledger. Refusal, toll skipping, and debt become Claim.
- Symbiote dependence teaches the organism what kind of help Hymn accepts.
- Baffles, plates, and hunters are infrastructure acting as pursuit logic.
- Echo notes and Operator procedures imply prior entries without explaining the clone cycle.

## First-Pass Mechanic Hooks

- Verb counters: fight, flee, harvest, file, mutate, consult.
- Merchant Claim ledger tied to refusals and resin tolls.
- Baffle control that lowers danger now and creates Smother Hunter risk later.
- Marked route streak that grants safety, then triggers Plate Snare.
- Dependence Marks from symbiote archive consulting.
- Field Report system: lower variance now, tailored enemy later.
- Leech Rental countdown: initiative now, ambush later.
- Spindle path repetition: micro-boon now, ending gravity later.
- Neutral Access: requires varied choices, selective reporting, limited mutation, limited avoidance, and disciplined use of procedures.

## Design Rule

New lore is accepted only if it can answer:

1. What can Hymn safely know now?
2. What is the hidden truth?
3. What gameplay pressure or option does this create?
4. Which ending or pressure axis does it touch?
