# Body Option Contract

This is the forward contract for mutations, symbiotes, pure-body discipline, and room branches.

The goal is not a long list of bespoke solutions. The goal is a small, stable set of body capabilities that rooms can recognize without exploding prose or content scope.

## Core Split

### Baseline Discipline

Baseline Hymn is always playable.

Pure-body choices should mean breath, timing, pain tolerance, tactical reading, restraint, footwork, leverage, and willingness to take narrower margins. They should not read as "no option."

Baseline discipline tends to:

- raise `baseline_discipline`
- preserve body boundaries
- earn recognition from witnesses that value clean conduct
- cost time, pain, injury, route risk, or missed power
- keep symbiotes and mutations from becoming mandatory keys

### Mutations

Mutations are reliable, always-on body identity.

They are usually weaker than symbiotes in the moment, but they are always present and reshape how Hymn moves, fights, reads rooms, is recognized, and is misrecognized.

A mutation must have:

- `body_option_type`: `mutation`
- `reliability_model`: `always_on`
- `capability_tags`: one to four stable tags used by rooms
- `tradeoff_axes`: pressure axes the option tends to move
- `in_encounter_use`
- `out_of_encounter_use`
- `surprising_second_use`
- `progression_identity`
- a visible weakness, social mark, recovery problem, appetite, misclassification, or route liability

### Symbiotes

Symbiotes are stronger, less reliable partners.

They are not equipment with wet names. They are living organisms with availability limits, harm states, appetite, preference, fear, memory, or partial agency.

A symbiote must have:

- `body_option_type`: `symbiote`
- `reliability_model`: active, conditional, passive-emergency, or similar
- `capability_tags`: one to four stable tags used by rooms
- `tradeoff_axes`: pressure axes the option tends to move
- `in_encounter_use`
- `out_of_encounter_use`
- `sapience_hint`
- `need`
- `relationship_pressure`
- `failure_modes`

Symbiotes can be more powerful than mutations because they can be absent, dormant, wounded, on cooldown, hungry, afraid, possessive, or unwilling to answer.

## Capability Tags

Rooms should target capability tags first, named options second.

Preferred tags:

- `speed`: enter, leave, or cross range before a room or enemy resets
- `burst`: one committed opening tempo, leap, or escape beat
- `cut`: sever armor, cords, limbs, bindings, or route tissue
- `brace`: hold a line, absorb impact, resist crush, endure a crossing
- `contact_punish`: make grapples, bites, and close pressure costly
- `contamination`: survive bad fluid, venom, rotwash, or foul air
- `read_damage`: read scars, old cuts, route marks, or wound history
- `calm_weak_life`: delay larvae, hatchlings, weak symbiotes, or nursery alarms
- `bargain_hunger`: negotiate with mouths, ledgers, toll organs, and food debt
- `identity_spoof`: lie to pulse, scent, rank, measuring rings, or body readers
- `seal_wound`: clamp, hold, open, or close wounds and pressure leaks
- `quiet_movement`: move without waking floors, eggs, brittle marrow, or guards
- `digest_unsafe`: convert unsafe biomass, eggs, grubs, or spoiled offerings
- `decoy`: leave skin, heat, scent, or a false grab point behind
- `anchor`: resist pull, current, forced movement, launch pressure, or displacement
- `barrier`: accept a strong hit through temporary living armor
- `scent_control`: alter initiative, recognition, predator attention, or social reading through scent
- `death_intercept`: redirect fatal damage into a symbiote or sacrificial body process

Do not multiply tags for every prose idea. If a new room needs a new tag, add it only when it will support several future rooms.

## Room Branch Rule

A room should be built in this order:

1. Pure-body route: playable, costly, and characterful.
2. Capability branches: one or two tags that change how the scene plays.
3. Named option flavor: only when a specific mutation or symbiote has a special relationship to the room.

Player-facing prose should stay contained.

Do not list every compatible mutation. Write one concrete branch such as "use a cutting body," "answer with a scent lie," or "brace into the pull." The runtime or generation notes can name the exact options that qualify.

## Tradeoff Standard

A good body option changes at least two things:

- combat spacing or commitment
- traversal or route choice
- social/ecological recognition
- future pressure axis
- vulnerability, appetite, cooldown, wound, or debt

Examples:

- A scorpion-tail style option extends reach and lets Hymn threaten narrow lanes, but it struggles in clinch range and leaves recognizable puncture marks.
- A symbiotic arm blade cuts harder than a grown blade, but it may sleep after feeding or pull Hymn toward fights it wants to finish.
- Bone plates let Hymn hold a line and pass crushing rooms, but wardens classify her as armor-bearing and heavier routes learn her.
- Remaining pure can win recognition as disciplined restraint, but the margins are narrower and pain debt accumulates.

## Generation Requirements

Forward scenarios should include body-option thinking in designer metadata:

- which baseline discipline route exists
- which capability tags matter
- whether any branch is mutation-favored
- whether any branch is symbiote-favored
- what tradeoff axis changes when that path is used

Do not make mutations or symbiotes keys to puzzles. They are martial identities, tactical shortcuts, ecological lies, social tells, and pressure multipliers.
