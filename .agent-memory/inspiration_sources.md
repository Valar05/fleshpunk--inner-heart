## Current Research Stack

The forward creative backbone is **martial anatomy**: mutation as combat specialization, movement philosophy, evolutionary adaptation, and survival optimization.

Use `.agent-memory/fleshpunk_corpus_research_stack.md` as the main corpus map for atmosphere, combat intelligence, biological adaptation, progression structure, and roguelike procedural design.

- Atmosphere and world tone: sword and sorcery danger tempo, weird hostile-world traversal pressure, ruined science-fantasy ecology, survival atmosphere, and mythic ruin aesthetics.
- Pre-1930 pulp/proto-pulp: use verified public-domain works for danger tempo, lost-world structure, expedition pressure, duel pacing, and mythic adventure clarity. See `.agent-memory/fleshpunk_research_pulp_before_1930.md`.
- Combat intelligence: HEMA structure, spear/sword/polearm geometry, measure, tempo, line control, leverage, commitment, recovery, pressure fighting, clinch behavior, counters, and readable injury logic.
- Biological adaptation: arthropod armor and limbs, cephalopod flexibility and camouflage, predatory insect strike systems, deep-sea sensory and pressure adaptations.
- Progression structure: breakthrough cadence, hierarchy pressure, iterative mastery, adaptation loops, and long-term identity change.
- Roguelike systems: systemic hazards, item economy, encounter pacing, class or mutation differentiation, attrition, and traversal pressure.

Restrictions: synthesize and abstract. Do not imitate prose, reuse copyrighted characters, borrow franchise terminology, or transform source material into close paraphrase.
