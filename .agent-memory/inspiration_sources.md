# Inspiration Sources

Use these as structural inspiration only. Do not copy names, exact phrasing, characters, or recognizable scenes.

- TV Tropes: structure for dilemmas, ironic costs, escalating consequences, cursed bargains.
- SCP Foundation: containment logic, anomalous object behavior, clinical framing inverted into body horror.
- Public-domain weird fiction: Poe, Machen, Blackwood, Chambers, early Gothic and decadent fiction.
- Medical anatomy and pathology references: organ names, tissue behavior, symptoms, surgical verbs.
- Industrial safety manuals: warning language, lockout procedures, pressure systems, contamination risks.
- Religious apocrypha and martyr stories: sacrifice, relics, forbidden cures, bodily vows.
- Roguelike event design: Slay the Spire, FTL, Caves of Qud-style risk/reward choices.
- Biotech and parasitology references: symbiosis, immune response, infection tradeoffs.
- Brutalist architecture and subway maintenance language: rooms as infrastructure, not just scenery.
